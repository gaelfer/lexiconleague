# Lexicon League Story Mode — World-Building Plan

## Creative foundation

- Tone: cozy mystery with adventurous combat.
- Hero: a village-born Inkling whose Resonant Ink can restore damaged meaning.
- Blotlings: abandoned meanings given unstable form; some may eventually be restored.
- Chapter One mystery: a masked thief uses a Blotling attack to steal the First Dictionary's definition of “meaning.”

## Chapter One milestones

1. **Archive Road attack** — protect the village and glimpse the thief.
2. **Evidence trail** — inspect the torn page and establish that the attack was a planned theft.
3. **Inkwell Archive** — investigate the First Dictionary, Vellum's notes, and the founding mural.
4. **Living village hub** — replace the hallway rhythm with connected civic areas, changing NPC dialogue, and optional clues.
5. **Pokémon-style building exploration** — every important exterior doorway leads to a compact interior; entry pauses and preserves the outdoor scene, interiors have a consistent exit threshold, and each building contains story, character, or mechanical value.
6. **Three investigations** — Archive, Market Fountain, and Scriptorium clues can be completed with limited ordering freedom.
7. **Community gathering** — rescued residents react to completed investigations and optional discoveries before Wordwood.
8. **Wordwood departure** — the player leaves with an emotional reason, a suspect, and an unanswered question.

## Building roadmap

- **Inkwell Archive** — story-critical; First Dictionary investigation. Implemented.
- **Player Home** — personal history, missing family member, future-dated letter. Implemented.
- **Scriptorium** — village craft, damaged-word repairs, missing silver quill. Implemented.
- **Market shops** — recovery quests, purchases, and merchant relationships.
- **Mayor's Hall** — civic records, political tension, and restricted evidence.
- **Resident homes** — optional character scenes that change after major events.
- **Mara's House** — optional cartographer's home; family maps show how the village grew around forgotten words. Implemented.
- **Mossbell Tea Room** — optional social interior; menus, regulars' cups, and a community noticeboard reveal village routines. Implemented.

## Rules for every interior

- The doorway must be visually recognizable without a floating building-name label.
- Entry and exit use a brief fade and place the player at a predictable threshold.
- Outdoor combat, opened seals, and defeated enemies remain preserved.
- Every room contains at least one interaction that reveals character, history, or a useful clue.
- Important interiors should visually change as the story advances.
