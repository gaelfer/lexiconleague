# Connected opening areas

- `/story/1`: The Road to Inkwell. A woodland crossing, survey shelter and orchard
  detour lead through three vocabulary seals to the eastern gatehouse. The hub’s
  homes and Archive are not duplicated here.
- `/story/village`: Inkwell proper. Its northern Wayfarer Gatehouse connects to
  the road and Wordwood. The village unlocks when the three road seals are cleared
  and the player enters the gatehouse.
- `/story/2`: Wordwood. Restore the three describing words from the survey notes,
  test them at the central monument, then walk seed → sprout → bloom across the
  stones. Walking around the middle stone is part of the sequence puzzle.

Press E at an outdoor gatehouse. Inside, walk through a labelled exit. Returning
through the entry resumes the paused outdoor scene; a different exit loads the
destination area at its gatehouse. The Story Map remains an optional shortcut.

Road answers and Wordwood's notes, words, tested solution, partial stone sequence
and sanctuary opening persist in the existing chapter checkpoint slots. Old valid
Wordwood checkpoints remain readable; already-completed Chapter 2 saves keep the
sanctuary unlocked. Fragment recovery records completion without blocking further
exploration with a full-screen victory overlay.

Development-only visual review entries:

- `/story/1?roadReview=camp`
- `/story/2?sceneReview=stone` (also bridge, burrow, sanctuary)
- `/story/2?arrival=gatehouse&sceneReview=entry`

Wordwood review mode does not write puzzle checkpoints. Normal arrival URLs do.
Later, unbuilt chapters are not yet part of this three-area gatehouse network.
